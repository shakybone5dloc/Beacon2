Privacy policy

Last updated November 19, 2026

Beacon is a job-search tracker in an invite-only beta, run by one person. This page says what Beacon keeps about you, why, who else sees it, and how to get it out or delete it.

What Beacon keeps

  • Your account: your email address, the name you give, and your password, stored only as a one-way hash that can't be turned back into the password.
  • What you put in: applications and their notes, statuses and dates, companies, interviews, stories, study cards, saved searches and the postings they find, and the files you upload or save, such as resumes, cover letters and copies of job postings.
  • People you add: recruiters, hiring managers, interviewers and other contacts, with whatever you record about them (a name, a title, an email address, a phone number, notes).
  • Your API keys: keys you add for AI providers and job sources, encrypted before they're stored. Beacon never shows them back to you in full.
  • Settings and usage: your preferences, such as the address you send email from and the weekly email, and a count of AI requests made with your keys so you can see your spending.

Why

Only to run the features you use. Beacon has no ads, sells nothing about you, and doesn't use your data to train anything.

Who else sees it

  • AI providers, when you use an AI feature: fit scores, cover letters, interview briefs, study cards, resume tailoring, check-in emails and answer grading send the text they need (for example a posting and your resume) to OpenAI or Anthropic, using your own key and under that provider's own terms and privacy policy.
  • Job sources, when you search: your search words and filters go to the job sources you search. Saving a posting fetches the page you link to.
  • Email, when you turn it on: the weekly email is sent through an email service. Emails you write in Beacon, like a check-in, open in your own mail app or Gmail for you to send; Beacon doesn't send them.
  • The person running Beacon can reach the server and its database to keep it running, and looks at your data only when needed to fix a problem or when you ask for help.

Nothing else is shared, unless the law requires it.

Cookies and your browser

Beacon uses cookies only to keep you signed in and to protect forms from forgery. Your browser also remembers a few preferences on your device, like light or dark mode and your last job search. There are no analytics or tracking cookies.

Where it's kept and for how long

Your data is stored on Beacon's server and kept until you delete it or your account. The server makes a backup every night; backups are kept for 14 days and then deleted.

Your choices

  • Download everything: Settings · Account · Download my data gives you a zip of everything you've put in, including your files.
  • Delete your account: Settings · Account · Delete your account removes your account and everything in it straight away. Copies in backups are gone within 14 days.
  • Change or remove anything you've added, at any time.

People you add

When you record details about other people, keep to what you need for your job search, and delete them when you no longer do.

A beta

Beacon is still being built. When this policy changes, the date at the top changes too, and anything significant will be announced in the app.
